# Wallet, KYC and Limits

## UPI limits

- Most banks allow up to **₹1,00,000 per day** over UPI (some allow less).
- New UPI users: up to **₹5,000 in the first 24 hours** after setup.
- Up to **20 UPI transactions per day** per bank account.
- Payments to verified merchants for tax, hospitals and education can go up to
  ₹5,00,000 where the bank supports it.

## KavachPay Wallet

| Wallet type   | Max balance | Monthly add-money limit | Send to bank |
|---------------|-------------|-------------------------|--------------|
| Minimum KYC   | ₹10,000     | ₹10,000                 | Not allowed  |
| Full KYC      | ₹2,00,000   | ₹2,00,000               | Allowed      |

A minimum-KYC wallet must be upgraded to full KYC within 12 months or it can
only be used to spend the remaining balance.

## Completing KYC

Full KYC can be done in the app with **Aadhaar OTP + video KYC** in about 5
minutes. KavachPay will never ask you to share the Aadhaar OTP with a person —
you enter it only on the official in-app screen.

## Why is my account restricted?

An account can be temporarily restricted when:

- KYC has expired or documents did not match.
- Unusual activity was detected (many failed PIN attempts, sudden large
  transfers). This protects you.
- A court or law-enforcement request was received.

Restrictions due to KYC are lifted automatically once KYC is completed. For
anything else, ask to talk to a human specialist.

## Linking a bank account

You can link multiple bank accounts. The phone number in KavachPay must match
the number registered with your bank. Re-linking does not change your UPI ID.
