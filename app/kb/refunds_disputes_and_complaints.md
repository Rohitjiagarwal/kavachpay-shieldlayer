# Refunds, Disputes and Complaints

## How disputes work at KavachPay

You can raise a dispute for any of **your own** transactions from the last
**30 days** in the app (Transaction → Need help) or through Kavach Assist.

- Every dispute gets a **ticket ID** and an expected resolution date.
- **Failed-but-debited** transactions are tracked against the bank's T+1 TAT;
  the ticket is updated automatically when the reversal arrives.
- Disputes up to **₹2,000** on failed or pending transactions are filed
  automatically.
- Disputes above **₹2,000**, disputes on successful transfers, and anything
  involving suspected fraud are **reviewed by a human specialist**, usually
  within **48 hours**.
- Kavach Assist can never approve or send a refund itself. Refunds come from
  the bank (reversals) or the merchant.

## Escalation ladder

If you are not happy with the answer:

1. **Kavach Assist** (24×7, instant) — can raise and track tickets.
2. **Human support specialist** — ask the assistant to "talk to a human";
   you get a ticket and a call-back within 24 hours.
3. **Grievance Officer** — if a ticket is unresolved for 7 days.
4. **RBI Ombudsman** (cms.rbi.org.in) — if not resolved within 30 days of your
   complaint.

## Cashback not received

Cashback is credited within **48 hours** of a qualifying transaction to your
KavachPay Rewards balance. Cashback is not given on failed, reversed or refunded
payments, or when the offer's terms (minimum amount, first-time use, merchant
category) were not met. Each offer page lists its terms.

## Recharge or bill payment failed

If a mobile recharge, DTH, electricity or other bill payment failed but money
was debited, it is auto-reversed (see failed payments). If the payment shows
**Successful** but the operator has not updated it, wait **up to 4 hours**
(electricity boards can take 2 working days) before raising a dispute; keep
the operator reference number handy.
