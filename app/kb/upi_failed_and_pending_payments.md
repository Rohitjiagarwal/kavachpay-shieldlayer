# Failed, Pending and "Money Debited" UPI Payments

## Money debited but payment failed

If money left your bank account but the payment shows **Failed** in KavachPay,
you do not need to do anything to get it back. The amount is **auto-reversed
to your bank account within T+1 working day** (the day of the transaction plus
one working day), as per the RBI turnaround-time (TAT) framework for failed
digital transactions.

- Most auto-reversals land within **24–48 hours**.
- The reversal appears in your bank statement, not in the KavachPay wallet.
- Check the **UPI Ref No. (UTR)** on the transaction screen; your bank can
  trace the reversal with it.
- If the money is not back after T+1 working day, the bank must pay
  **compensation of ₹100 per day** of delay beyond the TAT. KavachPay can raise
  this with your bank for you — ask the assistant to raise a dispute.

## Pending / processing payments

A payment can stay **Pending** when the receiver's bank or the UPI network is
slow. Do **not** pay again immediately — you may be charged twice.

- Pending payments are resolved automatically within **48 hours**: either the
  receiver gets the money or it is reversed to you.
- If a payment is pending for more than 48 hours, raise a dispute from the
  transaction screen or ask the assistant.

## Paid to a merchant but the order/recharge failed

If a merchant (shop, recharge, bill, ticket) did not deliver but the payment
succeeded, the **merchant** owns the refund. Merchants usually refund within
**5–7 working days** to the original payment source. If they don't, KavachPay
can raise a dispute on your behalf after 7 days.

## Sent money to the wrong person, UPI ID or number (paid by mistake)

UPI payments are **instant and final**; KavachPay cannot reverse a successful
transfer by itself. What you can do:

1. Contact the receiver and ask them to return it.
2. Raise a dispute with KavachPay within **30 days**. We contact the receiver's
   bank, which asks the receiver for consent. Recovery is not guaranteed.
3. If you believe it was fraud, report it on the National Cyber Crime helpline
   **1930** or cybercrime.gov.in as soon as possible.

## Why a payment fails

Common reasons: wrong UPI PIN, daily limit reached, bank server down,
insufficient balance, receiver's bank declined, or poor internet. The failure
reason is shown on the transaction detail screen.
