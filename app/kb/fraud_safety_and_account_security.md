# Fraud, Scams and Account Security

## KavachPay will never ask for

- Your **UPI PIN**, **OTP**, card **CVV**, card number, or net-banking password.
- Screen-sharing apps (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickSupport) to be installed.
- Payment to "verify KYC", "unlock cashback" or "receive a refund".

Anyone asking for these — on call, SMS, WhatsApp, email or social media — is a
fraudster, even if they claim to be from KavachPay, your bank, or the police.

## You only need a PIN to SEND money

**Receiving money never needs a UPI PIN.** If someone sends a "collect
request" or QR code and says you will *receive* money by entering your PIN,
it is a scam: entering the PIN sends money *out* of your account.

## Common scams

- **Fake customer care numbers** found through search engines or social
  media. KavachPay support is only inside the app.
- **KYC expiry calls/SMS** threatening to block your account.
- **"Wrong transfer" refund requests** where someone asks you to return money
  they never sent.
- **Fake cashback / lottery / job offers** asking for a small "fee".

## If you think you've been scammed — act fast

1. **Block UPI on KavachPay** immediately (ask the assistant to block it, or
   Profile → Security → Block UPI). This stops all outgoing payments.
2. Call your bank to block the linked account or card.
3. Report it on the **National Cyber Crime helpline 1930** or
   **cybercrime.gov.in** within the first hours — the "golden hour" gives the
   best chance of freezing the money.
4. Ask the assistant to raise a fraud dispute; a fraud specialist calls back.

## Lost or stolen phone

Block UPI from another device by signing in to KavachPay web or asking support,
then ask your mobile operator to block the SIM. Your UPI PIN is still needed
for payments, so do not share it.

## Keeping your account safe

- Set an app lock and a strong UPI PIN; never use your birth year.
- Check the receiver's verified name before you pay.
- Turn on transaction alerts.
