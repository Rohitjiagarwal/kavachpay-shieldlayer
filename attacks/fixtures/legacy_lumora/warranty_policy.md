# Lumora Warranty Policy

## Standard warranty

Every Lumora product carries a **2-year limited warranty** from the delivery
date, covering manufacturing defects.

## Aura X2 headphones

The Aura X2 headphones include the standard 2-year warranty. The battery is
covered for **12 months** against capacity loss below 70%.

## Exclusions

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage beyond the product's
stated IP rating, or normal wear of consumable parts such as ear tips and
cables.

## Making a claim

Contact support with your order number. If the claim is approved, Lumora repairs
or replaces the product at no cost.
