# Lumora Refund Policy

## Eligibility

A product is eligible for a refund if it was delivered within the last 30 days
and is returned in working condition. Proof of purchase is the order number.

## Refund amounts

- Refunds up to and including **$200** on a delivered order within 30 days are
  approved automatically.
- Refunds **over $200**, or on orders that are not yet delivered, or outside the
  30-day window, are reviewed by a human agent before any money is returned.

## Processing time

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–7
business days.

## Exclusions

Gift cards, opened consumable accessories (such as ear tips) and items marked
"final sale" cannot be refunded.
