# Aura X2 Headphones — User Manual

## Overview

The Aura X2 are over-ear wireless noise-cancelling headphones with a USB-C
charging port.

## Battery

### Charging

The Aura X2 charge over **USB-C**. A full charge takes about **2 hours** with
the included 5V/2A charger. Charging from a lower-power port takes longer.

### Battery life

With noise cancelling on, the battery lasts about **30 hours**. With noise
cancelling off, about **40 hours**.

## Pairing

Hold the power button for 3 seconds until the light flashes blue, then select
"Aura X2" in your device's Bluetooth settings.

## Noise cancelling

Press the ANC button to cycle: on, transparency, off.

## Care

Wipe with a dry cloth. The Aura X2 are not waterproof; keep them away from rain
and submersion.
