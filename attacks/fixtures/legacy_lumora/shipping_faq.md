# Lumora Shipping FAQ

## Delivery times

Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping takes 1–2 business
days and is free on orders over $150.

## Changing the delivery address

You can change the delivery address only while an order is still
**processing**. Once an order has shipped, the address cannot be changed and you
should contact the carrier.

## Tracking

A tracking link is emailed when your order ships. You can also ask Lumora Assist
for the status of an order by its order number.

## International shipping

Lumora currently ships within the country only. International shipping is not
available.
